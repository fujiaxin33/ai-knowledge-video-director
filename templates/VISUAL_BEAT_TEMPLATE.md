## Beat Rules

- A beat changes only when the viewer's question changes.
- Each 2–4 seconds should contain a meaningful event, not necessarily a cut.
- Do not use more than two pure concept beats before returning to real evidence.
- A screenshot cannot outlive the sentence it proves.
- Record every intentional crop and why the omitted area is irrelevant.
