# Stable Layout System

Use a small family of repeatable stages. Do not invent a new composition for every beat.

## Stages

### Presenter Stage

Human plus one supporting information region. Use for Hook, chapter bridge, important viewpoint, and Ending. Full Presenter is temporary, not the default teaching state.

### Motion Canvas

Full-screen concept/state animation; Presenter hidden or a fixed 12%–17% circular/rounded PIP. Motion is the main visual and must progress through states.

### Screen Stage

Real software is primary; Presenter is a fixed 14%–18% PIP only if it adds trust without obscuring content.

### Screen Focus

Real screen full-width with semantic annotation. Hide Presenter when space is needed for reading.

### Compare Stage

Two aligned states with a shared baseline and explicit correspondence. Reveal one side, then the other, then lock the relationship.

### Result Stage

The completed, real result plus a short conclusion. Hold long enough to read; do not replace the result with decorative celebration.

## 16:9 base grid

- Canvas: 1920×1080 at 30fps.
- Outer safe margins: at least 64px left/right and 48px top.
- Preferred editorial content gutter: 96–112px.
- Bottom 930–1040px is subtitle priority; teaching annotations should remain above it.
- Lock title, app badge, labels, and content windows to shared horizontal/vertical axes.
- Ordinary Presenter PIP: 268–346px wide (14%–18%), fixed lower-left or lower-right.

## 9:16 base grid

- Canvas: 1080×1920 at 30fps.
- Conservative safe margins: 72px left, 120–160px top, 280–320px bottom; reserve the platform interaction rail on the right.
- Use a readable contained 16:9 screen plus brand background, or a justified context-to-focus sequence.
- Never make a 16:9 information screen tiny only to claim it is complete.

## Face safe zone

Treat the face, eyes, mouth, head silhouette, neck, important shoulder line, and key hand gesture as protected. Cards, captions, badges, PIP, progress, and annotations do not overlap it. If space is insufficient: move the component, reduce nonessential content, use a dedicated screen stage, or delete it.

## Continuity

- Human first is a trust principle, not a screen-area quota.
- Keep the Presenter corner fixed across ordinary teaching states.
- Keep title and content baselines stable across related states.
- A layout change must correspond to a change in the viewer's task.
- Endings should continue the established information stage when a new Presenter composition would feel discontinuous.
