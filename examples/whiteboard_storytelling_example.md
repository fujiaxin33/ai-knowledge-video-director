# Whiteboard Storytelling Example

## Brief

- Topic: Why a sea breeze forms
- Audience: general viewers
- Mode: `WHITEBOARD_STORYTELLING`
- Knowledge goal: unequal heating creates pressure differences and moving air
- Story shell: a kite on the shore looks for what keeps pushing it

## Progressive scene

1. Hand draws the shoreline and sun.
2. Land gains heat marks; sea remains visually cooler.
3. Air arrows assemble from parts and begin to move.
4. The kite reacts to the moving air.
5. Knowledge Hero writes `冷热差 → 空气流动` and holds alone.

## Comedy punch

```text
Setup: kite believes a hidden person is pushing it
Anticipation: it turns around slowly
Pause: empty beach
Impact: one strong gust bends it backward
Reaction: kite stares at the new airflow arrows
Knowledge job: the reaction leads into pressure-driven air movement
```

## Contracts

- Sun, shore, arrows, and knowledge relationship draw progressively.
- The gust is an intentional motion/impact event, not fake handwriting.
- Drawing SFX follows pen-down intervals; the gust whoosh hits the bend frame.
- Caption is one short semantic line: `冷热差推动空气`.
- A human still approves whether the story is engaging and the comedy lands.
